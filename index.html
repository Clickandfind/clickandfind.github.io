About Phomab

Phomab is a free website for finding houses and rooms to rent in Bulawayo, including Nketha, Magwegwe, Luveve, Pumula and Cowdray Park. Tenants say what they need, landlords list their vacancies with photos, and Phomab matches them so they can chat and agree on the rent.
